## 4. Test-coverage assessment

**Strong.** ~7,200 lines of tier-0 tests over 21 files, byte-exact golden IR *and* plan regression (incl. the window region), an independent-oracle fuzz sweep that reaches `schedule()`, exhaustive refusal-code coverage for the window gate, locale tests, overflow tests at the Rational/clock/slab level. The five prior defects in this area (R-26/27/28, R-43/44, N-ir-schedule-1/2) all have verified fix evidence.

**Gaps, in priority order:**
1. **Multirate × merge is a blind spot** (IS-1): the sweep is lockstep-only by design, every merge fixture is lockstep, and the ratio sweep has no merge.
2. **Window regions mixed with ordinary channels** (IS-2): no test combines a window-scoped region with a channel outside it; the prover's endpoint model for window members is exercised by nothing that could falsify it.
3. `is_collective`/`to_cstring` enumeration gates are stale for `ExternalDelivery` (IS-8).
4. `ClockSet`'s window-length override — the load-bearing "a window member's clock is the window, not its substep" rule — has no direct unit test; IS-7's overlapping-window first-wins is consequently invisible.
5. `E-SCHED-WINDOW-CAUSALITY` is tested only against hand-built instruction streams; no integration test drives `lower()`→`schedule()` into a causality refusal.
6. `E-IR-MERGE-ANCHOR` remains unfirable (IS-13, KNOWN via R-43) — acceptable per pass-2's recorded decision, but re-flag the moment an anchor-producing rule is designed.
